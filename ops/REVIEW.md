# Browser verification review — launch profile `repair-core`

Reviewer: adversarial browser verification (fresh eyes, no prior exposure to the build).
Target: production build at http://127.0.0.1:3000, profile `repair-core`, in-memory enquiry store.
Date: 2026-10-06.
Read first: CANON §16, §17, §29; BUILD_COMMAND §3.1–3.3, §29; BUILD_PLAN §3, §5, §10.
Evidence root: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-HuksyTechRepairs-V2/fed608fe-e4d0-5214-b9d7-2c11e05758f0/scratchpad/` (`shots/` for screenshots, `html/` for saved view-source, `*.mjs` for the scripts that produced them).

## Verdict: FIX THEN SHIP

The site is Bench Pop, not a template: white/ink canvas, one signal orange used as three or four big blocks per page (the "Something else" tile, the second-diagnosis band, the primary buttons), Bricolage display at hero scale, mono sticker tags, problem-first tiles, an original mascot in exactly the places the plan allows, and copy that could not be lifted onto another repair shop's site ("Already been told it's dead?", "Something weird? If it has a motherboard, ask us.", "The worst answer is 'not this one'."). Mobile is designed rather than squashed at 360, 320 and 768; nothing overflows; body copy is 17.3px with 16px lists; focus rings are a 3px ink outline plus a 6px orange halo and are visible on every control I tabbed to; axe reports zero violations on ten pages at 360px; every flagged-off division returns a real 404 with the branded page and leaves no trace in the HTML, sitemap, robots or JSON-LD; the enquiry works end to end with JavaScript off. It does not ship as-is because the enquiry's anti-spam time brake silently discards a *valid* submission made under 2.5 seconds after the contact step renders and then tells the person they already saw a confirmation they never saw, with no way back to the form. Browser autofill makes that timing realistic on the one conversion the site has. One more item is a trust problem rather than a bug: the public Repair terms and Privacy pages render their unknowns as visible "to be confirmed by Husky" lines under a DRAFT banner, which contradicts the plan's "unknown = absent" rule. Everything else is minor.

## Checked and passed

Modularity / flagged divisions
- `/business`, `/business/schools`, `/privacy`, `/privacy/grapheneos`, `/refurbished`, `/refurbished/build`, `/recycle`, `/knowledge`, `/track`, `/about` all return HTTP 404 with the branded "didn't survive the drop test" page and `robots: noindex`.
- View-source of `/`, `/repair`, `/repair/*`, `/motherboard-repair`, `/book`, `/contact`, policies: no "refurb", "recycl", "knowledge", "GrapheneOS", "/privacy/" strings. "track" hits are the booking progress CSS class names; "business" hits are the two trade sentences on `/motherboard-repair` ("say you're a business") plus the policy's "business's practices". No "coming soon", "lorem", "placeholder" text (the word appears only as the labelled photo-slot component class), no `TODO` in rendered text.
- `sitemap.xml` lists only `/`, `/repair`, six category pages, `/motherboard-repair`, `/book`, `/contact`. `robots.txt` allows all, disallows `/book/done`. JSON-LD is `Organization` (name, URL, logo, areaServed Brisbane) plus `Service` for enabled divisions only plus `BreadcrumbList`; no address, hours, phone, ratings or reviews.
- Forged enquiry parameters (`intent=refurbished`, `intent=business`, `help=watches`, `step=99`, `logistics=pickup`) all fall back to step 1 quietly; logistics in this profile offers only "Arrange it with me when you reply" with no mail-in, pickup or drop-off copy.

Invented facts (against BUILD_PLAN §3 where only the trading name and "Brisbane" are confirmed)
- No prices, hours, address, phone, email, turnaround, same-day claims, warranty periods, certifications, review counts, star ratings, customer counts, years in business or partner logos anywhere in the rendered HTML. "$" matches were React Flight payload tokens. "years" appears only in battery/dust explanations. "warranty" appears only in the console FAQ about manufacturer warranty and in the draft policy (finding 2).
- Slop-phrase sweep (world-class, cutting-edge, seamless, premium, industry-leading, trusted, expert, best, secure, genuine): zero hits in rendered text.

Visual (Bench Pop vs template), judged on `ops/screenshots/M2–M5` and my own 360/768/320 captures
- No gradient blobs, glassmorphism, icon-card rows, pill-button rows, fake logos/metrics, stock photos or decorative grids. Photo spots are labelled shadow-box slots with a mono "PHOTO · 4:3" tag and a brief; none are faked.
- Orange is used as blocks (one tile, one band, buttons, mono counters), ink text on orange throughout (5.6:1 per the token test). Dark bands appear with a content reason (motherboard proof, final CTA, explainer) and are not alternated mechanically.
- Mobile: single column, full-width 56px choices, 44px header targets, hero stacks with stickers under the slot; 768 uses a two-column tile grid and keeps the desktop header. 320px: no horizontal scroll on `/`, `/book`, `/motherboard-repair`, `/repair/phones`.
- Mascot appears only on the logo, 404, home final CTA and enquiry confirmation.

Accessibility
- axe (wcag2a/aa, 2.1, 2.2 aa, best-practice) at 360px: 0 violations on `/`, `/repair`, `/repair/phones`, `/motherboard-repair`, `/book` step 1, `/book` step 6, `/contact`, `/policies/privacy`, 404, `/book/done`.
- Keyboard: first Tab is a working "Skip to content"; focus order header → content → footer; every focused control shows a 3px ink outline plus 6px orange halo (cream outline on the dark explainer). Radio groups move with arrow keys; the explainer chips select on ArrowRight and the board highlight follows (`shots/hires-chip-focus.png`, `shots/hires-header-cta-focus.png`, `shots/focus-book-radio-m360.png`).
- Mobile menu opens by Enter on `<summary>`, is a `<nav aria-label="Menu">`, links are reachable in order, and it closes on navigation; works with JavaScript disabled.
- Body copy 17.26px/26.75 (Inter), lists and choices 16.08px, mono labels 13px only for eyebrows/tags. Muted text `#5C5955` on white and `#B8B3AA` on ink both ≥ 4.5:1.

Enquiry (`/book`)
- Six GET steps with state in the URL; each empty submit shows a summary and the inline error for that step only (`shots/book-err-step1..3.png`, `shots/book3-empty-wait3s.png`); name/email preserved after a bad-email error; HTML in the brand field is escaped in the summary; the symptoms step keeps the ticked boxes and description on error.
- A valid submission (after the 2.5 s window) stores and shows a real reference (`HUS-001001` … `HUS-001005` across my runs), the reply email, next steps without any turnaround promise, and the magnifier mascot; the done page is `noindex, nofollow` and re-renders on reload.
- Entire flow completed with JavaScript disabled, including the POST, yielding `HUS-001005` (`shots/jsoff-done-m360.png`).
- Honeypot filled → silently dropped (correct for bots). Rate limit after five from one address shows "That's a lot of enquiries from this connection. Please wait ten minutes and try again." with details kept.
- Cookies: `httpOnly`, `sameSite=lax`, `path=/book`, `maxAge` 1 h — matches the privacy policy's description.

Motherboard explainer (`/motherboard-repair`)
- Five chips (44px tall) drive the board highlight by CSS: the selected area's rect goes to 3px stroke / 18% fill / opacity 1 while others sit at 2px / 6% / 0.55; pixel-diff confirms the board changes for Power, Charging, Display and Data at 360, 768 and 1440 (`shots/explainer2-*`). Works with JavaScript off and with `prefers-reduced-motion`. All four notes stay visible regardless of selection.

Engineering hygiene observed from the browser
- No third-party scripts, fonts or trackers; fonts self-hosted and preloaded. No console errors on any route (only the preload warnings in finding 10 and the expected 404 resource log on the 404 page). The only failed requests are aborted `_rsc` prefetches, which are normal Next.js prefetch cancellations. All 32 distinct internal links resolve to 200. No `href="#"` or disabled-link placeholders.
